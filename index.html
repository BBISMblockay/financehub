One workspace for your company’s operations.

SILO brings business data and day-to-day work together for finance, operations, purchasing and marketing teams. Employees, contractors and agents of companies using SILO work in their company’s workspace, with access by invitation from an administrator.

Sales and reporting

Review sales trends and product performance, compare reporting periods, and organize saved reports into dashboards for your team.

Inventory and purchasing

Review inventory, maintain product records, and build purchase orders with supplier, cost and shipment details to keep purchasing work in one place.

Finance and reconciliation

Review sales and payout records, reconcile operational data with accounting records, and prepare journal entries for human review before posting.

Marketing performance

Compare campaign spend and results from connected advertising accounts alongside sales reporting. Review performance over time to inform your team’s decisions.

Planned TikTok Ads reporting: read campaign spend, impressions, clicks and purchase metrics from a company’s authorized ad account for internal reporting. Availability is subject to TikTok approval and connection setup.

About SILO

SILO is an operations workspace operated by Baseballism. It supports internal business work for Baseballism and other companies using SILO. Workspace access is invitation-only; this public overview is available without an account.

For product questions, support or an invitation request, email support@get-silo.com. Read our Privacy Policy for details on how SILO handles company data and connected services.
