SILO — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

SILO is an internal operations platform owned and operated by Baseballism (“we”, “us”). It is used only by our own authorized personnel. SILO has no public sign-up, no consumer users, and is not offered as a product to anyone outside our company. This policy explains what data SILO holds, why, and how it is protected.

Who this policy covers

People who use SILO are our employees, contractors, and agents. We do not collect data from members of the public through SILO, and SILO does not display advertising, set advertising cookies, or track users across other websites.

What we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy

Personnel data Performance review content, compensation requests Internal HR processes, visible only to the specific managers and approvers involved
Connected-service data Sales, inventory, payouts and orders from Shopify; accounting records from QuickBooks Online; campaign metrics from advertising platforms Reporting, reconciliation, and preparing accounting entries

Intuit and QuickBooks Online data

Where a QuickBooks Online company is connected to SILO, we access it under OAuth using the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope, and only for the connected company. Specifically:

  • We read accounting records — chart of accounts, locations, reports, and transactions — to display them inside SILO and to reconcile them against our own operational data.
  • Where SILO writes to QuickBooks, it writes journal entries only, and only after a person has reviewed and explicitly confirmed each entry. Nothing is posted automatically.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted at rest, are never exposed to the browser, and are readable only by our server-side functions and by administrators of the connected company.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the internal accounting and reporting described here.
  • Disconnecting the integration in SILO deletes the stored tokens and the mirrored QuickBooks reference data.

How data is stored and protected

  • Data is held in a managed PostgreSQL database (Supabase) hosted on Amazon Web Services infrastructure in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Access is enforced at the database level by row-level security, scoped to the company a user belongs to. Being signed in is not sufficient to read another company’s data.
  • Credentials for connected services are restricted to administrators and to server-side functions.
  • Authentication is by email and password with sessions managed by our auth provider. Access is granted by invitation and revoked when an engagement ends.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with the infrastructure and service providers required to operate SILO — our database and authentication host, our transactional email provider, and the connected services listed above — and only to the extent needed to provide those services. We may disclose data where required by law.

Retention

Business records are retained for as long as needed for operational, accounting, and legal purposes. Account records are retained while a person has access and for a period afterwards for audit and attribution. Connected-service tokens are deleted when the integration is disconnected.

Your choices

Because SILO is an internal tool, people who use it can ask us to review, correct, or delete their account information, subject to our record-keeping and legal obligations. Contact us using the address below.

Children

SILO is a workplace tool and is not directed to, or usable by, anyone under 16.
