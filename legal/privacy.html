SILO — Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

SILO is an operations workspace operated by Baseballism (“we”, “us”). Companies use SILO to bring their sales, inventory, purchasing, finance and marketing data into one place. This policy explains what data SILO holds, why, and how it is protected.

Who this policy covers

SILO is used by the employees, contractors and agents of companies that use SILO (“customer companies”). Access is by invitation from a customer company’s administrator; there is no public sign-up and no consumer use. SILO does not display advertising, set advertising cookies, or track users across other websites.

This policy also covers people who submit the public early-access interest form. Submitting that form does not create a SILO account or workspace.

Early-access interest

When you join the early-access queue, we collect your name, company name and email address to review your interest and contact you about SILO early access. These details are visible only to SILO platform administrators and the service providers needed to operate the queue. They are not shared with other customer companies or used to enroll you in an automated marketing mailing list.

We retain these details while handling your early-access interest. You can ask to review, correct or delete them by emailing support@get-silo.com.

Each customer company’s data is kept separate. A user can see only the data of the company they belong to.

Google user data

A customer company’s administrator can choose to connect the company’s own Google accounts. SILO requests only these permissions, and uses them only to show that company its own reporting inside SILO:

  • Google Analytics (analytics.readonly) — read-only access to website sessions and conversions for the company’s properties.
  • Google Search Console (webmasters.readonly) — read-only access to search clicks, impressions and positions for the company’s websites.
  • Google Ads (adwords) — campaign spend and results for the company’s ad accounts. SILO only reads reporting data; it does not create, change or delete campaigns, ads or budgets.

How this data is handled:

  • It is stored in SILO’s database, encrypted in transit and at rest, and is visible only to users of the company that connected it.
  • OAuth tokens are readable only by SILO’s server-side functions and by administrators of the connected company. Removing the connection in SILO’s Integrations page deletes the stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the Google account’s security settings.
  • We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to build profiles. We do not transfer it to others except as described under “Sharing” below, to provide SILO’s features to the company that connected it, or where required by law.
  • SILO’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Intuit and QuickBooks Online data

Where a QuickBooks Online company is connected to SILO, we access it under OAuth using the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope, and only for the connected company. Specifically:

  • We read accounting records — chart of accounts, locations, reports, and transactions — to display them inside SILO and to reconcile them against that company’s own operational data.
  • Where SILO writes to QuickBooks, it writes journal entries only, and only after a person has reviewed and explicitly confirmed each entry. Nothing is posted automatically.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted at rest, are never exposed to the browser, and are readable only by our server-side functions and by administrators of the connected company.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the accounting and reporting described here.
  • Disconnecting the integration in SILO deletes the stored tokens and the mirrored QuickBooks reference data.

How data is stored and protected

  • Data is held in a managed PostgreSQL database (Supabase) hosted on Amazon Web Services infrastructure in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Access is enforced at the database level by row-level security, scoped to the company a user belongs to. Being signed in is not sufficient to read another company’s data.
  • Credentials for connected services are restricted to administrators and to server-side functions.
  • Authentication is by email and password with sessions managed by our auth provider. Access is granted by invitation and can be revoked by the customer company.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with the service providers required to operate SILO, and only to the extent needed to provide their service:

  • our database, hosting and authentication provider (Supabase, on Amazon Web Services);
  • our transactional email provider, for invitations and notifications;
  • our AI provider (Anthropic), when a user asks SILO’s assistant a question: the relevant results of that company’s own data are sent to answer it. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models under its commercial terms;
  • the connected services listed above, when SILO reads from or writes to them on the customer company’s instruction.

We may disclose data where required by law.

Retention

Business records are retained while the customer company uses SILO and as needed for operational, accounting and legal purposes. Account records are retained while a person has access and for a period afterwards for audit and attribution. Connected-service tokens are deleted when the integration is disconnected.

Your choices

People who use SILO can ask to review, correct, or delete their account information, subject to the customer company’s and our record-keeping and legal obligations. Contact us using the address below.

Children
