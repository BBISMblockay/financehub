Affiliate Platform Terms

Version 1 · effective 10 November 2026

These terms apply to every affiliate program run on SILO. You accept them together with the program terms of the brand that invited you, when you join that brand’s program. Where the two differ on how a program works (rates, dates, payouts, brand rules), the brand’s program terms apply.

1. Who you are dealing with

Your affiliate agreement is with the brand that runs the program (the “Brand”). SILO provides the software the Brand uses to run it: your signup, your portal, your codes and links, and the records of your earnings. The Brand decides who joins, sets the commission, and pays you.

2. How you earn

  • You earn commission on orders that use the discount code the Brand gave you, within that code’s active dates, at the rate in the Brand’s program terms.
  • Your affiliate links apply your code at checkout. An order is credited once, by its code, however the customer arrived.
  • Orders that are refunded, cancelled or found to be fraudulent do not earn commission, and commission already recorded for them is reversed.
  • Your portal shows your earnings as the Brand’s records stand. The Brand may hold or adjust amounts as its program terms allow.

3. Payment and taxes

  • The Brand pays your commission, by the method and schedule in its program terms. Payment obligations are the Brand’s.
  • You are an independent party, not an employee or agent of the Brand or of SILO.
  • You are responsible for your own taxes. The Brand may ask for tax information (for example a Form W-9 or W-8) before paying you.

4. How you promote

  • Clearly disclose your relationship with the Brand wherever you promote it, as the law where you and your audience are requires (in the United States, the FTC’s endorsement guides; for example “#ad” or “I earn a commission”).
  • Make only truthful claims about the Brand and its products.
  • Do not send spam, buy traffic that misrepresents the Brand, bid on the Brand’s name in search ads, or post your code on coupon or deal sites, unless the Brand’s program terms allow it.
  • Use the Brand’s images and materials only to promote the Brand, and stop when asked.
  • Keep your portal link private. It gives access to your account.

5. Your information

To run the program, SILO stores, on the Brand’s behalf: your name and contact details, your profiles and handles, your codes and links, the orders credited to you (without the customer’s details), your earnings and payouts, and a record of your acceptance of these terms (the name you signed, the version, the time, your IP address and browser). The Brand can see this information. It is used only to run the program and is handled as described in the SILO privacy policy. Ask the Brand to correct or delete your information; some records may be kept as the law requires for payments.

6. Ending

You may leave a program at any time by telling the Brand. The Brand may pause or end your participation as its program terms allow, including for breaking these terms. Commission you earned before the end is handled under the Brand’s program terms.

7. Changes

These terms are versioned. If they change, you will be asked to accept the new version before it applies to you.

8. The platform

SILO is provided “as is”. To the extent the law allows, SILO is not liable for the Brand’s program, its payments, or losses arising from your promotion of the Brand.

Questions about a program go to the Brand that invited you.
