Quick tips

  • Use a clear subject — it becomes the row title in the queue.
  • Set priority when something is time-sensitive or legal.
  • Attach a scan when you can; the team can work from the queue without chasing paper.
  • Unassigned items stay in the shared queue for Finance to route.
