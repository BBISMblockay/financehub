How this forecast works

Saved Plaid current balances anchor tomorrow’s forecast. Posted bank transactions count even when uncategorized or excluded from coding. Pending and removed transactions are omitted. Credit cards, loans and investments are shown for context, outside cash totals; their bank payments affect cash.

Trends use completed days from each account’s first observed transaction in the selected history through yesterday. Identified transfers are excluded from trends. Short histories can overstate a run rate; estimates do not predict seasonality or payment timing. Recurring plans replace their category’s trend in the same direction during their active dates. One-time plans are additional. Plans apply to all cash accounts in the company’s base currency. No currency conversion or ledger posting occurs.
