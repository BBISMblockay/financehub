How this forecast works

Saved Plaid current balances anchor tomorrow’s forecast. Posted bank transactions count even when uncategorized or excluded from coding. Pending and removed transactions are omitted. Credit cards, loans and investments sit outside cash totals. Linking a bank movement to a card or another account updates both sides in the liquidity table.

Trends use completed days from each account’s first observed transaction in the selected history through yesterday. Identified transfers are excluded from trends. Short histories can overstate a run rate; estimates do not predict seasonality or payment timing. Recurring plans replace their category’s trend in the same direction during their active dates. One-time plans are additional. Overrides replace the full category and direction total over their saved dates, even when you change the column view. A cashflow-group override takes precedence over individual COA bucket overrides. Reset restores the underlying trends and plans. Assumptions use the company’s base currency; account-assigned movements also appear in that account’s view. No currency conversion or ledger posting occurs.
