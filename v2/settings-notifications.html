Direct notifications

These are addressed to a specific person by the record itself, so there is nothing to configure at company level:

  • Payment request receipts and paid notices — the person who submitted the request.
  • Compensation decisions — the manager who raised the request.
  • Mail routing — the assignee, or the submitter once it is done.
  • Sample updates — the assignee when one is set (email, plus a Slack DM where Slack is configured).
  • Performance reviews — the employee, then their manager on signature.
