    Shipment overview

    Manually logged updates · not live tracking. Search is shared with other tabs. Filters above and KPI totals apply to POs; the shipment status filter applies only here.

    Dates in bold come from the shipment's booking, which is shared with every other PO in the same container. A shipment with no booking uses its own dates.

    How quantities and export work

    One row per shipment, including unassigned shipments. Blank assigned quantities use the full PO line quantity, marked *; split shipments stay separate. Unknown quantities show —. CSV exports exactly the rows shown, with explicit quantity basis and blank unknown values. Formula-like text is prefixed with an apostrophe for spreadsheet safety.

    BOOKINGS

    Freight bookings

    One booking is one container or forwarder booking. Several POs usually ride on the same one, so its ETD, ETA and actual arrival are entered once here and every PO inside it reads them. A PO's own planned arrival date is left exactly as it was — the gap between the two is shown as the slip.

    Attach a shipment to a booking from the PO's shipment drawer (Open a PO → Shipments → Booking). Deleting a booking never deletes its shipments; they fall back to their own dates.
