How credit is given

  1. Start with Shopify's actual sales, including refunds.
  2. Look at the visits Shopify recorded before each order.
  3. Give the sale to the last channel the customer came through (not counting direct visits) within the look-back window.
  4. If no channel can be identified, leave the sale as Unattributed.
  5. Check every day's totals against Shopify.

Started is the first channel we saw in the window, which isn't always how they first found you. Helped is a channel seen in between. Closed is the last click. A one-channel journey counts as both started and closed.

Missing or pending visit data is never counted as Direct. Older orders refunded in this period are included.

This shows the path customers took. It doesn't prove a channel caused the sale.

Where the data comes from

Shopify orders and visit history, synced daily. Spend comes from the ad platform and Redo syncs.
