      What accepting does

      This is local opening history. It is never sent back to QuickBooks as a journal entry. Acceptance freezes the baseline and requires a review note.

      Does one account start later than the rest?

      An account can be brought into Silo after its first months were already categorized in QuickBooks — a provider backload does not mean Silo owns those months. Set the first day Silo may post an account and its earlier periods can no longer be approved or posted, they leave On Deck, and whatever Silo’s ledger recorded for them is reversed. Leave it empty and every period is Silo’s, which is how every account starts. Nothing here is sent to QuickBooks.

      What a saved year proves — and what it does not

      Saving a year copies your QuickBooks general ledger into Silo — transaction references, memos, counterparties and running balances — and checks each account against a trial balance at the same end date. Closing balances are normalized as debits minus credits, which is what the Difference column compares.

      • QuickBooks reports a trial balance for the fiscal year to date, so a year is the only window it can check in full.
      • Each snapshot stands alone. Overlapping windows are never added together.
      • A balance match checks that QuickBooks’ own reports agree with each other. It does not prove that every source document was retained.
      • Invoices, bills, payment applications and attachments are not archived by this step. Keep QBO connected until the remaining migration checks are complete.
      • One window of up to 366 days at a time, before your Silo start date. It reads reports from QBO and saves them in Silo, and creates no journal entry.
