Ledger ownership & migration scope

QuickBooks remains the official ledger during this phase. Silo retains your opening history and its own account identities. Transactions keep their existing human approval and QBO recovery controls.

Opening balances are not historical transaction detail. Open invoices, bills, reconciliation, period locks, reversals and independent Silo ledger roll-forward require the next accounting phase before switching book ownership.
