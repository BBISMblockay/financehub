QuickBooks Online

Connects the general ledger so Accounting Export can map to real QBO accounts instead of typed account names. Connecting pulls the chart of accounts; nothing is ever written to QuickBooks from here. Use a sandbox company until the mapping has been reviewed. Sandbox and production use separate Intuit key pairs — connecting production needs its own credentials and its own redirect URI registered at Intuit.
